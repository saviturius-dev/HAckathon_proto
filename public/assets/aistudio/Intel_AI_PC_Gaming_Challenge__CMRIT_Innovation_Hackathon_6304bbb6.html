Zero Emulation Tax: Why x86 Beats ARM Buyer's Remorse

ARM laptops (Snapdragon X / X2) rely on Microsoft's Prism emulation layer for traditional Windows apps—where niche college engineering software, drivers, VPNs, and some games can stumble or slow down. Intel Core Ultra runs native x86 cleanly with zero emulation headaches.

Honest Spec Check (No Marketing Fluff)

Microsoft requires 40+ NPU TOPS for Copilot+ features (Recall, Cocreator, Live Captions). Combined "Platform TOPS" (like 120 or 180) adds CPU + GPU + NPU together—check the dedicated NPU figure and ensure at least 16GB RAM. If you only play heavy 3D AAA games, pair Intel Core Ultra with a discrete GPU.
